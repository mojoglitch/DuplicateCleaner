DUPLICATE CLEANER - how to get the APK (no coding, no installs)

1. Create a free account at github.com and click "New repository" (name it DuplicateCleaner).
2. On the repo page click "uploading an existing file" and drag in the CONTENTS of this
   folder (including the hidden .github folder). Click "Commit changes".
3. Open the "Actions" tab -> "Build APK" -> wait ~3-5 minutes for the green check.
4. Click the finished run -> scroll to "Artifacts" -> download "DuplicateCleaner-APK".
5. Unzip, send app-debug.apk to your phone, tap it, allow "install unknown apps".
6. Open the app, tap Scan, grant "All files access", tap Scan again.
